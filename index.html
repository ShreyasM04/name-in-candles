Each letter is built from one market's real chart. Reading the letter left to right walks forward in time through that chart, and each candle drops into the next spot of the letter it roughly fits, within about a third of the size. Candles are never resized: green ones rise from the bottom of their spot and red ones hang from the top, wherever the market left them. So stems are uneven, edges are ragged and wicks cross gaps. Every letter uses a different market, and the picks change every minute as the market moves. Hover any candle to see where it came from.
